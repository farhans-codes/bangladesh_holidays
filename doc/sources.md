## Incorporated 2026 amendments

- 25 January: national election/referendum closures on 11–12 February. The industrial-worker-only 10 February leave is outside scope.
- 8 February: national 11–12 February closures retained; employer-selected compensatory working days for industrial workers are outside scope.
- 8 March: 18 March added as executive leave.
- 5 April: 13 April general leave applies only to Rangamati, Khagrachhari and Bandarban; optional outside those districts for the relevant community.
- 14 May: 25 May added; 23–24 May explicitly open. Existing 26–31 May entries retained.
- 7 October (notice 91): 22 October added; Saturday 17 October explicitly open.
- 7 October (notice 92): 7 November general leave added.

The 2027 annual notification was published on 5 November 2026. Its 20 May overlap (Buddha Purnima and Eid leave) is preserved.

## Applicability and uncertainty

- Annual lists include all five optional community groups; no community filter automatically grants leave.
- Regional general leave and optional leave are separate records, with mutually exclusive district conditions.
- Source lunar-dependent flags remain visible. This snapshot does not claim subsequent moon-sighting confirmation for every religious date.
- Annual optional-leave entitlement/approval conditions belong to the employer; the package only uses explicitly approved occurrence IDs.
- Not included: banking, school, court, essential-service and industry-specific schedules, or constituency-only election closures (for example Bogura-6/Sherpur-3 on 9 April and Thakurgaon-1 on 29 October). District-wide application would overstate those closures.
- Annual notifications do not establish a complete calendar for every sector. The three fixed government opening dates apply only when the caller enables that policy.
